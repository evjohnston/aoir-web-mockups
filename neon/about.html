About
AoIR

The Association of Internet Researchers is an academic association dedicated to the advancement of the cross-disciplinary field of internet studies: a member-based support network for critical and scholarly internet research, independent of traditional disciplines and international in scope.

Facts

  • Founded 1998
  • Annual meetings 27
  • AIR-L subscribers 7,500+
  • News posts 294

What we do

  • AIR-L, a free, open mailing list. Subscribe
  • The annual Internet Research conference
  • Special issues with ICS and Internet Policy Review
  • The Ethical Guidelines and Risky Research Guide
  • Book, dissertation and student paper awards
