Research
ethics

AoIR has an ongoing commitment to research on and about the internet conducted in an ethical and professional manner. The Ethics Working Committee has produced three major reports to help researchers make ethical decisions.

Committee chairs

Michael Zimmer (Marquette University), Ylva Hård af Segerstad (University of Gothenburg) and Anja Bechmann (Aarhus University).

The committee welcomes comments and suggestions for issues and resources to add.
