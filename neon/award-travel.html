Kelly Quinn
Travel Scholarship

To widen participation in its conferences, AoIR offers conference fee waivers and partial travel stipends of $500 each year. Scholarships are funded through donations from conference attendees.

They are open only to participants whose papers have been accepted through peer review. The number awarded depends on what the conference budget can sustain and on the quality of applications. Since 2024 the scholarship has been named for Kelly Quinn.

Details

  • ForParticipants with peer-reviewed papers accepted to the conference
  • AwardA conference fee waiver and/or a partial travel stipend ($500)
  • Funded byDonations from conference attendees
  • ApplicationsEarly summer; the 2026 deadline was 10 July

To apply

  • Have a paper accepted to the annual conference through peer review.
  • Apply through the form posted with that year's call for travel scholarships.
  • Decisions are made by the Executive Committee, on the advice of the Treasurer and the local organising committee.
