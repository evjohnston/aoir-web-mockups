AoIR Dissertation
Award

The AoIR Dissertation Award was established in 2012 to recognise the work of emerging scholars in internet research. Submissions are reviewed by a committee of scholars experienced in supervising dissertations.

In 2025, the year of AoIR's first conference in Brazil, the award was given in two language categories for the first time: English and Portuguese.

2026 winner

    Bots and Other Platform Inhabitants: Stories of Inter-Bot Governance, Automated Play, and Protest on Reddit

Details

  • ForDissertations filed in the previous calendar year
  • PrizeA cash prize, announced by August each year
  • Judged byA committee of experienced dissertation supervisors
  • NominationsEarly spring

To nominate

  • A nomination letter explaining why the dissertation deserves the award and how it contributes to internet research.
  • The graduate or their supervisor must be an AoIR member.
  • A PDF of the dissertation, fully defended and filed in the previous calendar year.
