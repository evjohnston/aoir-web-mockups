Best Student Paper Award

The Best Student Paper award is selected from submissions to the annual AoIR conference. Every accepted paper written entirely by students is considered.

Papers are reviewed by a committee of AoIR members, and the award is presented at the conference.

How to be considered
  • Submit a paper to the annual conference's call for proposals.
  • Every author must be a student at the time of submission.
  • If the paper is accepted, it is considered for the award automatically.
