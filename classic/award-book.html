Nancy Baym Annual Book Award

The Nancy Baym Annual Book Award recognises the best work in the field of internet studies, and highlights the breadth of work on the social and cultural dimensions of networked media.

Until 2013 it was known as the AoIR Annual Book Award. In 2014 it was renamed for Nancy Baym, in recognition of her many contributions to the Association.

Get Info
For:
A single or co-authored book of internet research published in the prior calendar year
Prize:
A cash prize and a presentation at the annual conference
Judged by:
Three eminent scholars in the field
Nominations:
Early spring; self-nominations from members are welcome
Eligibility
  • The book must be a monograph on a single topic. Edited collections are not eligible.
  • All of the book's authors must be current AoIR members when it is submitted.
  • Each book needs a nomination letter from a current member, explaining its contribution to AoIR's intellectual community and its strengths.
