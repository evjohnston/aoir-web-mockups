Ethics

AoIR has an ongoing commitment to ensuring that research on and about the internet is conducted in an ethical and professional manner. The Ethics Working Committee, made up of ethicists and researchers from various regions and countries, has produced three major reports to assist researchers in making ethical decisions.

Committee

Chairs: Michael Zimmer (Marquette University), Ylva Hård af Segerstad (University of Gothenburg) and Anja Bechmann (Aarhus University).

The committee welcomes comments and suggestions for issues and resources to add.
