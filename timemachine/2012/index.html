Call for AoIR conference hosts in 2029 and 2030

11SundayOct 2026

Posted by Association Coordinator in Announcements, Conference

≈ Comments Off

The 30th and 31st annual meetings of the Association of Internet Researchers are looking for host institutions and conference committees. If your institution is interested in hosting AoIR2029 or AoIR2030, please send an expression of interest.

Our next meeting is AoIR2027, 17–20 November 2027, in Rimini, Italy.

Nancy Baym Book Award 2026

17ThursdaySep 2026

Posted by Association Coordinator in Announcements, Awards

≈ Comments Off

The AoIR Nancy Baym Annual Book Award committee is happy to announce this year’s winner: Archiving Machines: From Punch Cards to Platforms by Amelia Acker. The runner up is Healthy Users: The Governance of Social Media Well-being by Niall Docherty.

We received nearly 30 nominations this year. Congratulations to both authors!
