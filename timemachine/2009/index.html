Call for Proposals to Host AoIR2029 and AoIR2030

October 11th, 2026

The Executive Committee of the Association of Internet Researchers is seeking statements of interest for hosting the 30th and 31st annual meetings of the Association. Institutions and committees who may be interested in hosting AoIR2029 or AoIR2030 should send an expression of interest.

Our next conference is AoIR2027, the 28th annual meeting, in Rimini, Italy, 17–20 November 2027. Thank you to everyone who came to #AoIR2026 in Mexico City, 14–17 October 2026.

2026 Nancy Baym Book Award

September 17th, 2026

The AoIR Nancy Baym Annual Book Award committee is happy to announce this year’s winner: Archiving Machines: From Punch Cards to Platforms by Amelia Acker. The runner up is Healthy Users: The Governance of Social Media Well-being by Niall Docherty.
