Good to know

The text comes from the current aoir.org. Links go to other mockup pages, or nowhere yet. Forms don't send anything.

To share a page with a particular background color, add ?bg= and a color code to the link, for example windowed/index.html?bg=ff5a36. Your color choice is only saved in your own browser.
