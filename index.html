AoIR web mockups

Two full-site directions, each nine pages, built from the feedback on the November 2026 round: windowed elements floating on a solid color, with the color pulled into the background instead of filling every block.

Windowed

A · Windowed

The "Join the people who study the internet" direction. Cream windows, mono headlines and yellow highlighter, floating on a solid backdrop.

  • Each window's title bar carries one small accent color, borrowed from the neon e-zine but used sparingly on white
  • Backdrop picker in the footer: cobalt, tomato, mint, lilac, pink, ink, original cream

Classic

B · Classic

The Mac Classic / OS 9 direction. Strict 1-bit black-and-white windows, pixel type, and desktop icons on a solid color desktop.

  • Windows can be dragged by their title bars and collapsed (windowshade) with the box at the right

Notes

To share a specific color, add ?bg= with a hex code to any page URL, e.g. windowed/index.html?bg=ff5a36 or classic/index.html?bg=0d97b0. The picker remembers your last choice in your own browser only.

Content is drawn from the current aoir.org. Links inside the mockups go to other mockup pages or to #. Forms don't submit. The logo is a stand-in drawn in SVG; swap in the real file before anything ships.
