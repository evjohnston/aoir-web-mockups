About AoIR.

The Association of Internet Researchers is an academic association dedicated to the advancement of the cross-disciplinary field of internet studies. It is a member-based support network for critical and scholarly internet research, independent of traditional disciplines and existing across academic borders. The association is international in scope.

What we do

  • Host AIR-L, a free, open-access mailing list with over 7,500 subscribers. Subscribe · list rules
  • Organise the annual Internet Research conference, one of the premier academic conferences in the field.
  • Publish selected refereed papers each year with Information, Communication & Society and Internet Policy Review.
  • Produce resources for scholars, including the AoIR Ethical Guidelines and the Risky Research Guide.
  • Give the Nancy Baym Book Award, the Dissertation Award and Best Student Paper.
