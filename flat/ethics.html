Research ethics.

AoIR has an ongoing commitment to research on and about the internet conducted in an ethical and professional manner. The Ethics Working Committee, made up of ethicists and researchers from many regions, has produced three major reports to help researchers make ethical decisions.

<aside id="committee">

Ethics committee chairs

Michael Zimmer (Marquette University), Ylva Hård af Segerstad (University of Gothenburg) and Anja Bechmann (Aarhus University).

The committee welcomes comments and suggestions for issues and resources to add.
